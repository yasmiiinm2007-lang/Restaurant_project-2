About our restaurant

PizzaHouse opened in 2014 with one oven and a short menu. We still make our dough in-house every morning and cook everything to order.

We buy vegetables from local farms, import our tomatoes and flour from Italy, and keep the room warm and unhurried, whether you're here for a quick lunch or a long family dinner.
